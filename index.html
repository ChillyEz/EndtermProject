Welcome to the Dota 2 Wiki

Dota 2 is a multiplayer online battle arena game developed by Valve. Two teams of five players compete against each other, with the main objective being to destroy the enemy Ancient.

Every player controls a unique hero with their own abilities and playstyle. Heroes become stronger during the match by gaining experience and gold, which can be used to purchase items.

Basic Gameplay

A typical Dota 2 match consists of several important stages. New players can focus on these basic steps:

  1. Choose a hero that fits your preferred playstyle.
  2. Learn the hero's abilities and basic mechanics.
  3. Collect gold and purchase useful items.
  4. Work together with your teammates.
  5. Destroy enemy structures and eventually the Ancient.
